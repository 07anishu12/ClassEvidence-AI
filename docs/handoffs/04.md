## 5. Decisions and rationale

- The initial rubric remains v0.1 and explicitly says it is a hypothesis, not organiser-provided ground truth or expert validation.
- Five dimensions retain the planning weights 25/25/20/20/10; technical usability is capped so recording quality cannot silently dominate pedagogy.
- Missing evidence is `null`/`not_assessable`; it is never a negative score.
- A label is eligible only when the three essential pedagogical dimensions are assessed and at least 70% of weight is assessable. Otherwise the scoring result carries limitations and no label for the later service layer to handle honestly.
- The domain layer requires evidence IDs for non-null dimension scores and uses deterministic thresholds. A future approved model cannot change weights, thresholds, or the final label.
